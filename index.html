Our story

A small café on Stasikratous Street

Olive & Oat began with a simple idea: the unhurried Cypriot morning, served with care.

Whether it's a quick freddo before a lecture or a long Sunday brunch with friends, there's always a seat waiting for you.

Who runs the place

Andreas and Eleni

Founder & Owner

Andreas Constantinou

Andreas grew up above his grandmother's kafeneio in old Nicosia.

“I want every guest to feel like they've walked into my grandmother's kitchen — just with better coffee.”

Head Chef

Eleni Petrou

Eleni learned to cook in her mother's village kitchen near Paphos.

“Halloumi, carob, honey — the island gives us everything. My job is not to get in its way.”
